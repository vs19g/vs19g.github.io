This paper reports the first direct measurement of the <sup>18</sup>F(&alpha;,p)<sup>21</sup>Ne cross section, covering center-of-mass energies from 2 to 4 MeV. The measurement was made in inverse kinematics at TRIUMF-ISAC, with a radioactive <sup>18</sup>F beam on the helium gas of the ANASEN active target.

Combined with earlier data and statistical-model calculations, the result gives a new reaction rate with much smaller uncertainties. With the new rate, <sup>19</sup>F production in models of asymptotic giant branch (AGB) stars rises by about 45% relative to the previous rate.

I worked on site at TRIUMF for this experiment, testing and setting up the silicon detectors and helping optimize the QDC parameters of the readout.
