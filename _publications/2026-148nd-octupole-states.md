This paper reports new data on <sup>148</sup>Nd from the <sup>150</sup>Nd(p,t)<sup>148</sup>Nd reaction measured at Florida State University. It observes 54 excited states up to 3.5 MeV, with a focus on the 0<sup>+</sup> states.

The results point to the third 0<sup>+</sup> band, rather than the second 0<sup>+</sup> state, as the better candidate for a two-phonon octupole band, a reading supported by *spdf* interacting boson model calculations. The paper suggests similar candidates in <sup>150</sup>Sm and <sup>152</sup>Gd, and proposes staggering in B(E1)/B(E2) ratios as a way to identify bands built on more than one octupole phonon.
