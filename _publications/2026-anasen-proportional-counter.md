This manuscript describes the conceptualization and implementation of a novel design upgrade for the internal tracking proportional counter within the **ANASEN (Array for Nuclear Astrophysics and Structure with Exotic Nuclei)** active target.

### Technical Highlights:
* **Design Innovation:** Introduction of a new internal tracking geometry, TWINE, to improve vertex reconstruction and background rejection. Counter-twisted anodes and cathodes cross at eight points, so the position is interpolated within one 39–46 mm cell instead of along the full 348.6 mm wire, an interval nearly an order of magnitude shorter.
* **Performance Metrics:** On alpha-source calibration, the crossover topology locates the vertex to 6.0 mm FWHM ($$\sigma$$ = 2.57 mm) at a source position and 10.7 mm FWHM pooled over five source runs, against 10–20 mm for the original counter: about a threefold improvement in position resolution.
* **Induced-Signal Validation:** A Shockley–Ramo weighting-field calculation, built on finite-element solutions of the counter's fields, predicts how the cathode charge fraction varies along each cell. On 9850 crossover events from alpha-source runs, the measured charge fraction oscillates with the predicted period and phase, with a single scale factor as the only free parameter.
* **Experimental Validation:** Details the performance of the upgraded detector during experimental campaigns investigating $^{27}Al(\alpha, p)$, $^{24}Mg(\alpha, p)$, and $^{17}F(\alpha, p)$ reactions at the John D. Fox Laboratory.
* **DAQ Integration:** Discusses the synchronization of digital signal processing with the proportional counter's timing response for high-rate experiments.

This work serves as a foundational technical reference for future $(\alpha, p)$ reaction studies using the upgraded ANASEN system.
