I am a nuclear physicist with a Ph.D. in Physics from **Florida State University**, where I defended my dissertation in November 2026 (degree conferred December 2026). My doctoral work was done at the **John D. Fox Superconducting Linear Accelerator Laboratory** under Prof. Ingo Wiedenhöver. My research sits at the intersection of nuclear structure, astrophysics, and advanced instrumentation.

### Research & Expertise
My dissertation, [*The study of (&alpha;,p) reactions using the upgraded ANASEN detector*](/publication/2026-phd-dissertation), studied $$(\alpha,p)$$ reactions that shape nucleosynthesis in X-ray bursts and core-collapse supernovae. I rebuilt the ANASEN active target around **TWINE** (Twisted Wire Instrument for Nuclear Experiments), a proportional counter whose counter-twisted anode and cathode wires give the position along the beam from their crossings and from the charge induced on the cathodes. I tested that induced-charge response against a Shockley–Ramo weighting-field calculation: the measured cathode charge fraction varies along each cell with the period and phase the calculation predicts. I then took the upgraded detector through two in-beam campaigns, $$^{27}\mathrm{Al}(\alpha,p)^{30}\mathrm{Si}$$ with a stable beam and $$^{17}\mathrm{F}(\alpha,p)^{20}\mathrm{Ne}$$ with a radioactive beam from RESOLUT. The $$^{27}\mathrm{Al}$$ analysis established what the upgraded detector can do and showed which settings have to change, and together with simulations it defines the beam energy and thresholds for the next measurement. The details are in [TWINE: Twisted-Wire Tracking and the Shockley–Ramo Signal](/portfolio/twine-shockley-ramo/).

Along the way I built expertise in:
* **Detector Development:** Designing, building, and calibrating gas-filled proportional counters and silicon strip arrays, from wire stringing to in-beam operation (the ANASEN active target).
* **Digital DAQ:** Integrating CAEN 1740 and 1725 digitizers into a single triggered readout with FSUDAQ, and setting up CoMPASS-based acquisition.
* **Simulations:** Finite-element field and weighting-field calculations with Gmsh, Elmer FEM, and ParaView, analysis in ROOT and C++, and drift and avalanche modeling with Garfield++ as the next step.

### Background
Before coming to FSU, I was a Junior Research Fellow at the **Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR)** in Mumbai, where I worked on digital signal processing for the **INGA array** and characterized $$LaBr_3(Ce)$$ detectors. I hold a dual degree from **BITS Pilani - Hyderabad** in Physics and Chemical Engineering, where my thesis focused on nanophotonics-enabled solar water desalination.
